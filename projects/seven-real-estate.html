Branding

SEVEN REAL ESTATE

A complete visual identity and branding project created to give Seven Real Estate a modern, premium and consistent image across digital platforms.

Year :

2026

Industry :

Real Estate

Client :

Seven Real Estate

Project Duration :

Ongoing

The Brief

Seven Real Estate needed a stronger and more consistent visual identity to support its positioning in the real estate market. The goal was to create a modern and premium brand image that could be applied across digital communication, social media and property marketing materials.

Creative Direction

I developed a visual direction focused on clarity, elegance and consistency. The branding system was designed to communicate professionalism while maintaining a contemporary and distinctive look across all brand touchpoints.

Brand Applications

The identity was applied across social media content, property presentations, promotional visuals and digital communication. Each asset was designed to maintain a consistent visual language while highlighting the agency’s properties and services.

Outcome

The project resulted in a more cohesive and recognizable visual presence for Seven Real Estate. The new branding provides a strong foundation for consistent communication across social media, digital campaigns and future marketing materials.
