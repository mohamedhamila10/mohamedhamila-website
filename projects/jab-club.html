JAB CLUB

Ongoing sports photography collaboration for JAB CLUB, documenting boxing and CrossFit sessions through dynamic, high-energy imagery.

PROJECT DETAILS

I worked regularly with JAB CLUB to photograph boxing and CrossFit training sessions, capturing the club’s atmosphere, movement and intensity through strong, dynamic and authentic imagery.

I focused on movement, expressions and training details to create powerful images that reflect the energy of the athletes and the intensity of each session.

The photography combines action shots, close-ups and environmental images, using contrast and composition to highlight strength, motion and the identity of the club.

The final photography series creates a bold and energetic visual identity for JAB CLUB, providing versatile imagery for social media, promotional communication and brand storytelling.
