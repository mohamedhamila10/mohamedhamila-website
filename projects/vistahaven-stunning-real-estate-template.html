PARAD’EYES

Product and model photography for Parad’eyes Optique, combining detailed eyewear shots with lifestyle imagery to present the collection in a modern and engaging way.

PROJECT DETAILS

I photographed both eyewear products and models for Parad’eyes Optique, creating a visual library designed to showcase the collection from both a product and lifestyle perspective.

I focused on lighting, reflections, composition and details to highlight the shape, materials and finish of each frame in a clean and polished way.

I photographed models wearing the eyewear to show how each piece looks in context, creating natural and stylish imagery that complemented the product shots.

The final photo series combined product and model photography to create a cohesive visual presentation for social media, promotional communication and digital use.
